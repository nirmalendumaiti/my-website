RESEARCH THEMES
Ecological Humanities Exploring human-nature entanglements, environmental crisis, and ecological ethics in Anglophone literature.
CURRENT RESEARCH

My doctoral research at Vidyasagar University investigates the intersection of Ecological Humanities and Anglophone Fiction, focusing on postcolonial ecocriticism and eco-geographical spaces. My paper at Resistance conceptualises 'Dumped Communities' and 'Living Garbage'.

Eco-geographical Spaces

Spatial intersection between place, culture, and environmental surroundings

Dumped Communities and Living Garbage

My paper questions: What if living species and communities are also treated as garbage and dumped?

Psychogeography and the Himalayan Hill Space

Analysis of the urban spaces of the hill stations in Darjeeling and Kalimpong through Sai's psychogeographical exploration in Kiran Desai's The Inheritance of Loss.

Eco-literary Ethics & Postcolonial Spaces

Examining environmental grief, borderlands, and non-human agency.
